Welcome to Bulb Word!

Guess the secret 5-letter English word in 6 tries.

How to play

  • Type a word using your keyboard.
  • Press Enter to send it and Backspace to delete a letter.
  • Your word must be a real English word.

💡 The lightbulb

Stuck? Click the lightbulb for a hint. You get only 3 hints per word:

  1. Part of speech: noun, verb or adjective.
  2. Category: animal, object, action, feeling or quality.
  3. The biggest clue: the meaning and the first letter.
